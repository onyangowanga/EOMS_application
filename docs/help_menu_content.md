
# Help Menu Content — EOMS (Events Operations Management System)

This document defines the complete help menu content for the EOMS application. It is structured for use inside your in‑app Help Center, Help Modal, or User Guide. It provides clear and simple explanations for all modules and features of EOMS.

---

# 📘 1. Getting Started

Welcome to EOMS — a platform designed to manage all operational and financial tasks during events such as funerals, weddings, fundraisers, conferences, and more.

### What you need to log in:
- Your phone number
- A one‑time OTP code (SMS/WhatsApp)

### After login:
Your dashboard shows the modules you have access to depending on your **role**.

---

# 🏠 2. Event Dashboard

The **Event Dashboard** gives you a quick overview of the event.

### You will see:
- **Operational Progress** — % of tasks completed across all subcommittees
- **Financial Progress** — funds collected vs funds spent
- **Subcommittee Overview** — progress of each committee
- **Cluster (Mobilisation) Summary** — fundraising progress
- **Approval Requests** — items waiting for your approval
- **Alerts and Deadlines** — overdue tasks, pending payments

---

# 👥 3. Committee Members

This module manages all people involved in the event.

### You can:
- View all members
- Add new members
- Assign roles (Chair, Secretary, Treasurer, Finance, etc.)
- Assign members to subcommittees
- Edit or remove members

Roles determine what a user can see or do.

---

# 🧩 4. Subcommittees

Subcommittees handle day‑to‑day tasks.

### In each subcommittee, you can:
- View tasks
- Assign tasks
- Track progress
- Add members
- Add estimated costs (automatically creates budget items)
- View budget and reports for that subcommittee

Each subcommittee has a **lead** responsible for driving progress.

---

# 📋 5. Tasks

Tasks represent work that needs to be completed.

### Each task has:
- Title and description
- Assigned member
- Deadline
- Progress %
- Status (To‑Do, In Progress, Blocked, Completed)
- Optional estimated cost (generates a budget item)

Updating task progress helps update the event’s overall operational progress.

---

# 💰 6. Budget & Finance Module

This module is for Finance & Budget Committee, Treasurer, and top officials.

### You can:
- View the full event budget
- Approve or decline budget items
- View expenditures
- Track remaining funds
- View allocations per subcommittee

Budget items come from:
- Subcommittee task cost estimates
- Manually created financial items

---

# 📝 7. Budget Items

Budget items represent financial needs.

They may be:
- **Auto‑generated** from tasks
- **Manual** budget entries from Finance Team

Every budget item requires approval before funds can be requested.

---

# 📨 8. Requisitions

After a budget item is approved, a committee may **request funds**.

### A requisition includes:
- Purpose
- Amount requested
- Linked budget item
- Needed‑by date

Requisitions require approval from:
- Chairman
- Treasurer
- Finance Committee Member

---

# 💵 9. Payments (Treasurer)

Once a requisition is approved, the Treasurer pays the funds.

### Treasurer can:
- View pending payments
- Pay suppliers or subcommittees
- Record payment details
- Upload receipts

All payments update the event’s financial progress.

---

# 🤝 10. Clusters (Mobilisation)

Clusters are fundraising groups.

### Cluster Leads can:
- Add contributions
- Log pledges
- View daily collections
- Submit funds to Treasurer

Treasurer must **confirm** cluster submissions.

---

# ✔️ 11. Approvals Center

This is where all approvals happen.

### Approval categories:
- Budget items
- Requisitions
- Payments
- Cluster submissions

You will **only see items that match your role**.

---

# 📊 12. Reports

The Reports module provides insights for decision‑making.

### Includes:
- Summary report
- Operations report (subcommittees & tasks)
- Finance report
- Cluster mobilisation report
- Member activity report

Reports can be downloaded as **PDF or Excel**.

---

# 🧑‍💼 13. My Profile & User Settings

You can:
- Update your display name
- Change default OTP method
- View your roles
- See committees you belong to

---

# ❓ 14. Frequently Asked Questions

### Why can’t I see some modules?
Your role may not permit access.

### I can’t log in
Ensure your phone number is correct and accessible.

### My updates are not visible
Try refreshing the page — some updates require a reload.

### I’m not receiving OTP
Try switching between SMS and WhatsApp.
